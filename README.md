# 🦠 Volunteer Delivery Game – District 5, Ho Chi Minh City (2021)

## 🧩 Introduction

In **2021**, the **COVID-19 pandemic** spread rapidly across the world with the emergence of the **Delta variant**.  
In **Ho Chi Minh City**, strict social distancing measures were implemented: starting from **May 31**, the city enforced distancing rules, and by **July 19**, **Directive 16** was applied citywide.

In **District 5**, residents were reminded daily to follow the **5K safety principles**:  
**Mask – Disinfection – Distance – No Gathering – Health Declaration.**

During the same time, the **national vaccination campaign** was accelerated, with many vaccine shipments delivered through **COVAX / UNICEF** to ensure timely protection for citizens.

You play as a **teenage volunteer** working on the **frontline of a quarantine zone (District 5, Ho Chi Minh City)**.  
Your mission is to **deliver essential supplies without direct contact**, **remind citizens not to gather**, and **help maintain safety and morale** during the lockdown.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Instructions

### 🕹️ Controls

| **Action** | **Key / Input** | **Description** |
|:------------|:----------------|:----------------|
| Move | `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` or Arrow Keys | Move character in four directions |
| Interact / Talk | `E` | Talk to NPCs or accept missions |
| Open Dialogue | `E` | Display story or task information |
| Pick Up Item | Walk over item | Automatically collect boxes or supplies |
| Drop / Deliver Item | Left-click on item slot | Place item on the ground for delivery |
| Pause / Menu | `Esc` | Open the main menu or exit screen |

---

### 💬 Accepting Missions

- Stand next to an **NPC (Non-Playable Character)**.  
- A **speech bubble icon 💭** will appear above their head.  
- Press **E** to start a **conversation** — the dialogue box will appear.  
- After talking, click **“Accept”** to take the mission.  
- Complete the given objective (e.g., collect or deliver supplies).  
- Once done, **return to the NPC** to report back and **confirm mission completion ✅**.

---

### 📦 Collecting Items

- Walk into **supply boxes or packages** to automatically pick them up.  
- Collected items are added to your **inventory**, displayed in the **top-right corner** of the screen.  
- You can collect multiple items as long as you have storage space.

---

### 🚚 Dropping or Delivering Items

- To **place or deliver an item**, **left-click** on the item slot in your inventory.  
- The item will be dropped onto the ground — this is necessary for completing delivery steps or missions that require item placement.

---

## 🧠 Reminders

- Always **wear a mask** and **keep a safe distance** from others.  
- Avoid **gathering** or staying too close to NPCs for long.  
- Complete all missions carefully to represent the **discipline and dedication** of real-life volunteers.  
- Each mission completed symbolizes another **contribution to your community** during lockdown.
